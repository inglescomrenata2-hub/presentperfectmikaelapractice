Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Grammar practice for Mikaela — already / just / yet / ever / never & specific past time
Quick reminder:
Present PerfectPast Simple
Used with already, just, yet, ever, never — no exact time given.
"Mikaela has already read three books this year."		 Used with a specific finished time: yesterday, last week, in 2021, when she arrived...
"Mikaela read that book last month."

Part 4 — Sentence production

Write your own original sentence for each prompt. Use the tense that fits the time expression.

2. (last weekend / do something) — Past Simple

4. (when you arrived in Ireland / your first impression) — Past Simple

5. (yet / visit a place in Ireland) — Present Perfect (question)

6. (ever / feel homesick) — Present Perfect (question + short answer)
